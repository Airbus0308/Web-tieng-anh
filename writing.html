Assessment Guide

🎯
TR — Task Response (25%) Address all prompt parts, develop supported arguments, and state a clear position throughout.
🔗
CC — Coherence & Cohesion (25%) Organize into 4–5 clear paragraphs with logical sequencing and natural linking devices.
📚
